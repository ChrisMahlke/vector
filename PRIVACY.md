# Furlong Ruler Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Furlong Ruler measures distances and areas on a map. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics of its own and no server of its own. This policy covers what leaves your device when you use it.

## Your location

If you allow it, Furlong Ruler uses your device's location while the app is open. It uses it to show where you are on the map, to center the map on you, and to show which way you're facing.

If you put a point where you stand, with "Measure from Here" or by touching and holding your own location, that point becomes part of your measurement like any other. In Road, Trail and Bike modes it is sent to Apple's Maps service to find the route, as described below.

If you record a route, Furlong Ruler keeps receiving your location while the recording runs, including with the screen off or another app open, and draws the route you take. It stops when you stop the recording. Recorded routes stay on your device.

Furlong Ruler doesn't send your location to anyone but Apple, and only for the purposes above.

If you use "See in AR", which places your points in the street through the camera, Apple's ARKit uses your location to download Apple's street imagery for the area around you and match it to what the camera sees. This happens only while that view is open.

## Camera

Furlong Ruler uses the camera only while you view a 3D model on a table or your points in the street, in augmented reality. Nothing the camera sees is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

## Motion

While a route is recording, Furlong Ruler reads your device's barometer to count how much you climb. The readings stay on your device.

## Your measurements

The points you place, your measurements and the measurements you save stay on your device. The measurement on screen is kept on your device so it's still there the next time you open the app. The name and total of your most recently saved measurement are shared with Furlong Ruler's widget, on your device.

Some features send points to Apple's Maps service:

- In Road, Trail and Bike modes, the two ends of each leg are sent so the line can follow roads, paths or bike routes. Freehand measuring sends nothing.
- When you share, save or export a measurement, its start and end points are sent to look up the names of the places there.
- Look Around asks Apple for street-level imagery of the point you've selected, or of each spot along the line as you walk it.
- "Directions in Maps" hands the point to the Apple Maps app.

## Elevation, relief and contours

The elevation profile, steepness shading, line of sight, 3D models and the Relief and Contours layers use terrain tiles from the AWS Terrain Tiles dataset, served free by Amazon Web Services' Open Data program. Furlong Ruler downloads the tiles that cover the area it needs and works out heights on your device: the line you measure is never sent. Like any website, Amazon Web Services receives your device's IP address and the tiles requested, which show roughly which area you're looking at; [Amazon's privacy notice](https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/) applies to those requests. Downloaded tiles are kept on your device so they needn't be fetched again.

## Search

What you type into search is sent to Apple's Maps service to suggest places, along with the area the map is showing. A coordinate you type is read on your device and sends nothing.

## Files you open

GPX and GeoJSON files you open in Furlong Ruler are read on your device and not sent anywhere.

## Maps

Furlong Ruler draws its maps with Apple's MapKit, which loads map data for the area on screen from Apple. Apple's handling of map, search and directions requests is covered by the [Apple Maps privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/) and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If you choose the Topo (US) map, its tiles for the area on screen are loaded from The National Map, run by the U.S. Geological Survey. Like any website, USGS receives your device's IP address and the tiles requested; the U.S. Geological Survey's own policies apply to those requests. The Relief and Contours layers load terrain tiles as described above. No other map sends anything to anyone but Apple.

Furlong Ruler uses no third-party code that collects data.

## Settings

Your settings, the map's last position and your chosen snap mode are saved on your device only.

## Sharing

When you share an image, a flyover or orbit video, a GPX file or a total, it goes only where you choose in the share sheet. A video is recorded on your device with iOS's screen recording, which asks your permission first. A 3D model is built on your device and stays there.

## Siri and Shortcuts

Requests from Siri and Shortcuts are handled on your device. Measuring to a place by name searches Apple's Maps service for that place, as search does.

## Children

Furlong Ruler doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
